Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Business Automation ("we", "our") provides Facebook automation tools. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: the username and contact details you provide in Settings.
  • Facebook data: Page IDs, Page access tokens, and public comment content needed to deliver auto-replies. Tokens are stored securely on your server and never shared.
  • Usage data: basic analytics such as pages visited and automation statistics, used only to improve the service.

2. How we use data

We use your data solely to operate the automation services you enable — replying to comments, scheduling posts, and showing your dashboard statistics. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing.

3. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies: a session cookie to keep you logged in, a theme preference, and your cookie-consent choice. See our Cookie Policy.

4. Data security

Access tokens and passwords are stored hashed/encrypted on the server with restricted file permissions. While no system is 100% secure, we follow industry-standard practices to protect your data.

5. Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data at any time via the contact details in the footer. Deleting a connected Page immediately stops all automation for it.

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above will reflect changes. Continued use of the service constitutes acceptance.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us via the contact details on our About section.
