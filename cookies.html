Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains how Business Automation uses cookies and similar storage.

1. What we store

  • Session cookie (sess): keeps you logged in to the dashboard. HttpOnly, expires after 30 days. Strictly necessary.
  • Theme preference (ba-theme): remembers your dark/light choice. Stored in your browser only.
  • Consent choice (ba-cookie-consent): remembers whether you accepted or declined cookies, so we don't ask again. Stored in your browser only.

2. What we don't do

We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or third-party analytics beacons. No cookie data is sold or shared.

3. Managing cookies

You can clear site data anytime in your browser settings — this logs you out and resets your theme and consent choice. The strictly-necessary session cookie is required for the dashboard to function; declining optional storage does not break the public website.

4. Changes

If we introduce new cookies, this page will be updated and the consent banner may re-appear.
